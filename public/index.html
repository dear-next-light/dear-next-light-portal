About Dear Next Light

A little curiosity.
A little brighter tomorrow.

Dear Next Light is an independent project creating small games, tools, and experiments around play, everyday life, technology, and a better future.

Made with curiosity, care, and the belief that even a small idea can open up something good.

Dear Next Light は、遊び・暮らし・テクノロジー・未来をテーマに、小さなゲームやツールを作る個人プロジェクトです。
少し先の未来を、少し明るくする。そんな小さなアイデアを、一つずつ。
